## Manuscript Status

This project is a **formal research paper** (`v1.0.0`) specifying a post-merge verification and auto-remediation framework for multi-agent programming environments. It is built on three formal definitions, three propositions, and three architectural pillars.

| # | Component | Version | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dynamic Conflict Window (Definition 1) | 1.0.0 | ✅ Defined |
| 2 | Post-Merge Verification Operator (Definition 2) | 1.0.0 | ✅ Defined |
| 3 | Auto-Remediation Bound (Definition 3) | 1.0.0 | ✅ Defined |
| 4 | Admission Independence (Proposition 1) | 1.0.0 | ✅ Proven |
| 5 | Monotonicity of Conflict Window (Proposition 2) | 1.0.0 | ✅ Proven |
| 6 | Minimum Tier Depth (Proposition 3) | 1.0.0 | ✅ Proven |
| 7 | Three Architectural Pillars | 1.0.0 | ✅ Specified |

No empirical work has been performed under this protocol. The empirical evaluation and simulation of AetherFlow against traditional merge queues remain to be conducted.
